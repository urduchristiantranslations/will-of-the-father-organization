For visitors abroad

Questions from
around the world.

What does your Christian ministry do in Pakistan?

Based in Lahore, we share the Gospel through outreach, church gatherings, Bible teaching, prayer, and baptism. We also serve children and families through practical care, including meals and support in brick-kiln communities.

Can a church or Christian organization abroad partner with a ministry in Pakistan?

We welcome inquiries from churches and Christian organizations around the world. Email us to learn about our work in Lahore and discuss prayer, staying connected, or possible ways to work together.

How can someone abroad ask about giving?

Our website does not process payments. Please email us before sending a gift so we can share current bank-transfer or Western Union instructions and help you confirm availability and fees with the provider in your country.
